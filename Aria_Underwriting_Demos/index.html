The Agent Harness, told as one underwriting decision

Maya Sharma asks Aria a deceptively simple question — "Should I refinance my auto loan?" Underwriting it safely forces every layer of the harness to do its job. Each demo below shows one layer working on that same journey, in the Contoso / Microsoft IQ theme.

Fabric IQ · accounts & cash-flow Foundry runtime · agent team Work IQ · risk & compliance Web IQ · live market rates
The underwriting journey (same spine in every demo)
Pull income & cash-flow→ Pull credit band→ Fetch live market rates→ Compute break-even & new payment→ Risk & fair-lending check→ Compose recommendation
